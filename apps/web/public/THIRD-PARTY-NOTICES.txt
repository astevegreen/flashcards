Onboarding starter decks: open data
===================================

The curated starter decks offered at first run (Korean Starter: Beginner Words; Mandarin Basics:
Common Characters) are built by scripts/build-starter-decks.mjs from the open data below, changed
only as described. Each deck also records its sources (the deck's source link, and the Notes field of
every card).

NIKL Korean learning vocabulary list (한국어 학습용 어휘 목록, 2003): KOGL Type 1 (공공누리 제1유형)
Source: https://www.korean.go.kr/front_eng/down/down_02V.do?etc_seq=70
Licence: https://www.kogl.or.kr/info/licenseType1.do
Used in: Korean Starter: Beginner Words (words, part of speech, Hanja origin, level, frequency order)

이 덱은 국립국어원에서 2003년 작성하여 공공누리 제1유형으로 개방한 '한국어 학습용 어휘 목록'을
이용하였으며, 해당 저작물은 국립국어원 누리집(https://www.korean.go.kr)에서 무료로 내려받을 수 있습니다.

This deck uses the Korean learning vocabulary list published in 2003 by the National Institute of
Korean Language (국립국어원) under the Korea Open Government License (KOGL) Type 1. Changes: level-A
(beginner) entries only, in the list's frequency-rank order; homograph numbers dropped; a word listed
under two parts of speech with the same meaning kept as one card; English meanings added from
Wikidata. The National Institute of Korean Language does not sponsor or endorse
this app.
